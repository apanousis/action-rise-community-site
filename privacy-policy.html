03

How we use it

  • Create and manage your account, and authenticate you securely.
  • Automatically sync eligible runs and validate event completion from your workout data.
  • Build and update your personal map — the 500-metre blocks you've discovered and their Bronze→Platinum tier.
  • Let you browse, register for, and pay for events, and process refunds when needed.
  • Calculate and track your points balance and let you redeem rewards.
  • Run groups, leaderboards, and achievements, respecting your privacy choices (e.g. opting out of the leaderboard).
  • Send event reminders and social notifications you haven't opted out of.
  • Issue invoices and keep the accounting records Greek tax law requires.
  • Detect fraud, secure the App, and respond to support requests.

We do not use your health or location data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

04

Legal bases for processing

Where GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases (Article 6 GDPR):

  • Contract — account setup, event registration, subscriptions, and the core map/points features you sign up for.
  • Consent — HealthKit/Google Fit access, location access, and push notifications, which your device asks you to grant separately. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings, which will limit the related features.
  • Legal obligation — invoicing and accounting records required under Greek tax law.
  • Legitimate interest — keeping the App secure, preventing abuse of the points/rewards system, and improving reliability.

05

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers ("processors") who help us run the App, under contracts that limit their use of your data to the services they provide us:

  • Clerk — authentication and identity (email, Google, Apple sign-in).
  • Stripe — payment processing for event registrations and subscriptions.
  • OneSignal — push notifications.
  • Apple (HealthKit) / Google (Google Fit) — the platform health/fitness frameworks your device uses to share workout data with the App, governed by Apple's and Google's own health-data rules.
  • Elorus — Greek-compliant invoice generation for paid events and subscriptions.
  • Supabase — hosts the App's database, where your account, activity, and event data is stored at rest.
  • Hetzner — cloud hosting for the App's backend services, on data centers certified to ISO 27001 (information security management) and ISO 9001. These backend instances are ephemeral compute — they don't hold a persistent copy of your data, which lives in Supabase instead.

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — in which case we'll take reasonable steps to ensure the same level of protection continues to apply.

06

International transfers

Our hosting provider, Hetzner, runs the App's ephemeral backend services on data centers located in the EU, so that infrastructure does not by itself involve a transfer of your data outside the European Economic Area. Our database, hosted with Supabase, and some of the other processors listed above, may still process or store data outside the EEA. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognized under GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision, to ensure your data stays protected.

07

Data retention

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep it for longer — most notably invoices and accounting records, which Greek tax law requires us to retain for a set number of years regardless of account status.

08

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit — all traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS (TLS), using current standard protocol versions and cipher algorithms; we retire outdated ones as they're deprecated.
  • ISO-certified hosting — our backend runs on Hetzner infrastructure, whose data centers are independently certified to ISO/IEC 27001 (information security management) and ISO 9001 (quality management).
  • Database security — the App's persistent data lives in Supabase, a managed Postgres database with encryption at rest and row-level access controls.
  • Access control — role-based access for our admin systems, so only authorized staff can reach user or payment data.
  • Verified webhooks — payment and authentication events (Stripe, Clerk) are cryptographically signed and verified before we act on them.
  • Session security — JWT-based authentication for API access.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately for its sensitivity.

Your rights

10

Children's privacy

Action Rise Community is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16, or the digital consent age set by your country's law if lower. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we'll delete it.
