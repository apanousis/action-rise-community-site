GDPR & your data rights

If you're in the EU or EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) gives you specific rights over your personal data. This page explains those rights and how to use them with Action Rise Community.

02

Health data gets extra care

The workout data the App reads from Apple HealthKit or Google Fit — your runs, distance, pace, and routes — counts as "special category" data under Article 9 GDPR. We only access it with your explicit permission through your device's health-data controls, we use it solely to power features you've asked for (auto-syncing runs, validating event completion, filling in your personal map), and we never use it for advertising or sell it to third parties. You can revoke HealthKit/Google Fit access at any time in your device settings — the App will simply stop receiving new workout data.

03

Your rights under GDPR

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — ask us to delete your account and personal data, subject to what we're legally required to keep (see Retention in the Privacy Policy).
  • Restriction — ask us to pause processing your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it transferred to another provider where technically feasible.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent — for anything based on consent (health data, location, push notifications), at any time, without affecting past processing.
  • Automated decision-making — Action Rise Community does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects, so this right does not currently apply to how we use your data.

04

How to exercise your rights

Email us at the address below with your request. To protect your account, we may need to verify your identity before acting on it. We aim to respond within one month, as required by GDPR; for complex requests, we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you if we do.

Many of these actions — like reviewing your profile, or deleting your account — are also available directly inside the App.

05

Legal bases, in short

We rely on contract (running the App's core features), consent (health data, location, notifications), legal obligation (invoicing under Greek tax law), and legitimate interest (security and fraud prevention). The full breakdown is in our Privacy Policy.

06

International transfers

Our backend services run on Hetzner's EU data centers as ephemeral compute, so that hosting doesn't involve a transfer outside the EEA. Our database, hosted with Supabase, and some of our other service providers, may still process or store data outside the EEA. Where that happens, we rely on safeguards recognized under GDPR — such as Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision — to keep your data protected. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

07

Lodging a complaint

If you believe we've mishandled your personal data, we'd appreciate the chance to put it right directly first — but you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As our company is based in Greece, that's ordinarily the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, though you may also contact the supervisory authority in your own EU/EEA country of residence.

Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα)

Website: www.dpa.gr
